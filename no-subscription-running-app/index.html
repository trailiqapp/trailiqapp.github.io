No subscription arithmetic

No monthly plan. No annual renewal.

A Trail IQ Full Access purchase does not renew. Apple handles the purchase through your App Store account, and Trail IQ includes a Restore Purchase action for an existing entitlement.

  • The current local price is shown before you confirm
  • Apple's purchase confirmation is required
  • There is no Trail IQ monthly or annual billing period
  • Updates remain subject to App Store availability and compatibility

No account required

A simpler purchase model matches a simpler data relationship.

Trail IQ is local-first, has no social feed and does not require a Trail IQ account. Full Access is associated with your App Store purchase rather than a recurring Trail IQ membership.
