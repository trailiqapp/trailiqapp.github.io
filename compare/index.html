Choose by priority

Start with the experience you actually want.

Choose Trail IQ if Your own route history is the coach

You repeat routes and want to know what is coming, where you usually gain or lose time, and what your history suggests doing next.

Choose Moment if Terrain-led sections come first

You want repeated-route coaching organised into short, terrain-defined Moments, with broad live sensor input and section-by-section guidance.

Choose Strava if Community motivates you

Clubs, challenges, leaderboards, segments and sharing are an important part of how you train.

Choose Apple Race Route if You want the native Watch option

You use Apple Watch and want a straightforward race against your last or personal-best effort.

The Trail IQ difference

Tell me what's coming, then tell me what my history says to do.

Trail IQ is built around repeated-route personal intelligence. It joins the route ahead with your previous execution, optional heart-rate context and what happened through recurring sections.
