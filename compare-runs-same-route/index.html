A faster time is only part of the story.

Before the workout

Know what your own history says about the route.

Choose a saved or familiar route and Trail IQ prepares the context that matters before you begin. The comparison stays within the same workout type, so running history is not mixed with walking or cycling.

  • Review the latest, quickest and typical matching efforts
  • See recurring strong and difficult route sections when enough history exists
  • Preview upcoming climbs and other meaningful route features
  • Choose a previous workout as an optional live Ghost

During the workout

See what is coming while the route is still unfolding.

Follow the assigned route, remaining distance and live metrics from the map-first workout screen. When you choose a Ghost, its marker shows where the earlier effort was at the same elapsed time.

  • Track your progress along the assigned route
  • Compare against a selected earlier effort with live Ghost
  • Receive optional route, segment and heart-rate coaching cues
  • Keep spoken cues, tones and haptics under your control

After the workout

Understand where the result was made.

The Workout Result brings the same-route story forward. See whether you finished ahead or behind, inspect where time changed and decide what is worth carrying into the next effort.

  • Compare against the previous matching workout
  • Review pace or speed changes by route section
  • Add heart-rate context when compatible data was recorded
  • Update Route Memory as reliable repeated efforts accumulate

Bring your route history

You do not always have to start from zero.

Supported GPX, TCX and FIT workout files can seed familiar-route history. Trail IQ checks for duplicates, keeps richer existing records and compares workouts only when the activity type and route evidence are suitable.

Local-first by design

Your personal route history stays personal.

Trail IQ does not require an account or upload your workout and health data to Trail IQ servers. Apple Health integration is optional, and Full Access is a one-time purchase rather than a recurring subscription.
